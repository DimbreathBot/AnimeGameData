<title name = Theft Denied, Murder Admitted? An Ongoing Investigation into the Missing "Star of the Snowfield"/>
<image preview name = UI_Img_SnezhnayaNewspaperPage_Pic_04/>
<center>Theft Denied, Murder Admitted? An Ongoing Investigation into the Missing "Star of the Snowfield"</center>
This paper reports:

The Snezhnaya Royal Railway Transport Bureau has announced the arrest of a suspect in the long-running investigation into the disappearance of the "Star of the Snowfield."

According to Officer Yudintsev, who conducted the interrogation, the suspect — identified here by the pseudonym V. Buyanov — entered the victim's residence in the early hours of the morning. He is alleged to have stolen the priceless jewel known as the "Star of the Snowfield" and murdered the victim, identified here by the pseudonym A. Valannikov.

During interrogation, Buyanov firmly denied stealing the jewel, but readily confessed to the killing.

"Yes, I killed Valannikov (pseudonym). He was a swindler and a loan shark. But I did not steal from him. That is not why I turned myself in. I came forward because I believed I had killed something greater than a man — though, apparently, I did not even manage the killing very well. What I killed was a principle. A principle! Do you understand? I confessed to the murder of my own free will, and yet you insist on accusing me of taking some ridiculous possession. You are a good man, but I will not accept that accusation."

Valannikov's (pseudonym) lady companion, however, rejected Buyanov's denial in heated terms:

"...If that murderer did not steal the 'Star of the Snowfield,' then who did? Never mind my poor Valannikov (pseudonym) for one moment — who else could possibly have taken it? In Her Majesty's name, send the man to the gallows at once! As for the jewel, simply record it as lost. There is no need to waste any more time searching for it... What I mean is, we should be thinking about Valannikov's (pseudonym) funeral, should we not? The murderer is already in custody. Compared with that, what importance could one or two missing jewels possibly have?"

Further updates will follow as the case develops.

<title name = A Triumph for Security in the Capital! "Wandering Samurai" Who Terrorized Citizens for Weeks Brought to Justice!/>
<image preview name = UI_Img_SnezhnayaNewspaperPage_Pic_02/>
<center>A Triumph for Security in the Capital! "Wandering Samurai" Who Terrorized Citizens for Weeks Brought to Justice!</center>
Exclusive Report: The shadow over Snezhnograd has finally lifted. The Inazuman "Wandering Samurai," whose violent rampage kept the capital in fear for weeks, was captured in a pre-dawn operation yesterday.
For weeks, a masked Kairagi had been striking late at night across Snezhnograd's Downtown District and along secluded paths beyond Glupov, leaving the city increasingly fearful as the number of victims grew. Eyewitnesses described him as wearing battered crimson armor and a bizarre mask. Combined with his uncanny swordsmanship, his frightening appearance was reportedly enough to make several victims drop their belongings and flee before he could say a word.
Following a coordinated investigation led by the Criminal Investigation Division and Druzhna patrol units under the personal supervision of Snezhnograd's Police Chief, the culprit was successfully apprehended late last night. The investigation proceeded without interruption, with officers working day and night until the suspect was located inside an abandoned bakery in the Downtown District. When the fully armed culprit attempted to leave the premises to commit another crime, the patrol teams stationed nearby immediately took action and subdued him!
Investigations reveal that the criminal was a licensed merchant from Ritou in Inazuma. Due to an irreconcilable conflict of interest between the mercantile association and the Kanjou Commission in charge of the area at the time, said association was rendered bankrupt two years ago. Unwilling to revert to being a common citizen, the criminal, backed into a corner, stole crimson armor and a sacred blade that had been offered to a local shrine, then fled the country. First, the criminal fled to the border region of Nod-Krai and engaged in rank charlatanry by assuming the status of an Inazuman Samurai. After being exposed by the Curatorium of Secrets, he then sneaked into Her Majesty's sacred capital, Snezhnograd, planning to continue his deceptions. However, after discovering that he could plunder at will by relying on his terrifying attire, the offender repeatedly employed the same tactic. He was eventually apprehended by law enforcement after inciting city-wide panic.
As the criminal is a fugitive, the Inazuman authorities have refused to recognize his expatriate status. As such, he now awaits judgment under Snezhnayan law. For further updates on how this case shall progress, please keep an eye out for the latest news.

<title name = Tales of the Strange and Incredible: The Jack Frost Witch (Contributor Story)/>
<image preview name = UI_Img_SnezhnayaNewspaperPage_Pic_09/>
<center>Tales of the Strange and Incredible: The Jack Frost Witch (Contributor Story)</center>
Contributor: Shagova (pseudonym)

I'm Shagova, an adventurer. A few days ago, I accepted a commission and set out for one of the most dangerous snowfields on the outskirts of Snezhnaya. (And yes, I went alone. Before you ask why I would venture into such a perilous place without anyone by my side, the answer is simple: I wanted the entire reward for myself! Besides, I'm a highly capable adventurer!)

Unfortunately, all my preparations proved useless when a sudden blizzard swept in and left me hopelessly lost in the snowfield. The cold was unbearable, and before long, I felt as though I might freeze to death. With no other options left, I remembered a story my grandmother had told me long ago:

"Child, listen carefully... If you ever find yourself lost in a vast, endless snowfield, recite that ancient incantation, and the Jack Frost Witch will appear from the depths of the storm. But remember, only when you are trapped in the darkest and most hopeless of situations may you call upon her name..."

It took me quite some time, but eventually, I managed to dig the incantation out from the depths of my childhood memories: "Haskratol-rabkadisumavits-kuporomgozinadek." (And believe me, I know how it sounds, but I wasn't simply making those words up! I have my own brain to thank for remembering such a tremendous amount of completely useless information!)

As it turned out, my grandmother's story was true after all. The Jack Frost Witch appeared before me, soaring through the storm on her broom, with a group of round and plump Jack Frosts trailing behind her. She rescued me from the snowfield and brought me back to her courtyard. Yet my troubles were far from over. The witch told me that she would send me home if I fulfilled her wish. But if I failed, she would turn me into flower fertilizer.

So, what exactly did the Jack Frost Witch want from me? A choir. More specifically, a Jack Frost choir. The problem was that the Jack Frosts had absolutely no interest in cooperating, and every attempt at singing together ended in complete chaos. Yet somehow, I was expected to turn them into a well-trained choir capable of singing together perfectly by the following morning.

Don't ask me how I managed it. All I can say is that I'm very grateful I wasn't turned into flower fertilizer.
(Unfortunately, despite making it deep into the snowfield, I still failed to complete my client's commission. My reward... gone...)

Still, I wanted to share this spell for summoning the Jack Frosts with everyone. Perhaps it may save your life someday!

"Haskratol-rabkadisumavits-kuporomgozinadek!"

Though, I should warn you, it may also result in you becoming flower fertilizer. On the bright side, you might just be lucky enough to hear a well-trained Jack Frost choir perform a beautiful harmony together!
