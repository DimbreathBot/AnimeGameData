I often find myself missing that dress with the tassels, the heavy bed curtains, and the first rays of sunlight that would stream into the manor each morning.
But it's alright now — if I narrow my eyes just a little, the light seeping through a crack backstage feels just like the one in our manor.
I know that life is gone forever, and I can miss it all I want, but I also feel that what I have here now is no less than what I lost.
The fantastical sets designed by the prop master, the many fans who come all this way just to see me, and the quiet dancing girl who doesn't talk much — these are all mine! I won't lose any of it again! Never! Absolutely never!
