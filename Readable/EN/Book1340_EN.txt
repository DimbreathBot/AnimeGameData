Case File No.: GANO. F. Yz. Op. 1. D. 2
Subject: Concerning the Train Hijacking Case
Case Status: Closed

Report
On November 28 of this year, a train carrying crystal ore from Novokitezhgrad to various towns across Snezhnaya was hijacked in a coordinated attack.
All five hijackers posed as rail staff. After bypassing routine security checks through ██, they hid explosives inside the crystal ore and boarded the train. Twenty minutes after the train departed, they seized full control of the driver's cab. They demanded that the train be rerouted to Snezhnograd, where they planned to detonate the explosives and destroy the Kresnik's Torch.
After being hijacked, the conductor managed to send out signals. He also used maintenance and refueling as an excuse to stop at stations along the line and buy time for the Transport Bureau. This move enraged the rebels, and the conductor was subsequently killed.
Eprokin, then Director of the Transport Bureau, boarded the train to negotiate, accompanied by his men and several civilian volunteers. Negotiations soon collapsed into violence, but the rescue party eventually overpowered the hijackers and brought the train to an emergency stop right before the Kresnik's Torch.
The hijackers were identified as members of a notorious fae rebel group. Under the name "██," the group had previously staged multiple attacks on humans across Snezhnaya.
The incident left five dead (the conductor and four hijackers), seven injured (four civilian volunteers and three Transport Bureau personnel), and two missing (one hijacker and one civilian volunteer).

Attachment: Confidential Report Concerning the Rebel Organization and Its Leader, the "Burner of Cities"
...Current intelligence confirms that rebel leader Natalia von Ostenwined-Cirnareka, codenamed the "Burner of Cities," went missing during the train incident, while all other core members of the group were killed in the Transport Bureau's rescue operation.
Natalia, the "Burner of Cities," was the granddaughter of Photius von Ostenwined, the second Grand Duke of the Vila. After Grand Duke Photius and all his sons died from sudden strokes, Natalia was in line to succeed him as Grand Duke. Glowatza Strivozha publicly backed her succession and proposed an engagement between her and Pyotr, a kinsman of similar age. However, Natalia's mother, Eleanor-Elina von Kamenenko, rejected the proposal. She voluntarily renounced her daughter's succession rights and brought her to the Cirnareka clan, where she married the son of the clan's leader at the time.
After the Cirnareka clan's lands were nationalized, Natalia took her adoptive father's surname as a banner and rallied the descendants of fae nobles who had lost their titles and estates in the Battle of Belovodye. They spurned Her Majesty Anastasya's benevolence and clemency and staged uprisings across Snezhnaya in the name of "Restoration."
Given that the Cirnareka clan no longer has a leader and the von Ostenwined line has died out except for Natalia, no further parties can be held accountable at this time.
Notably, the organization once included a Water Imp codenamed "the Sage." This individual claimed to be the direct grandson of the "Lackland" Vodyanoy Dombertiev. He also fiercely denounced the first Grand Duchess of the Water Imp, Vaselisa Vodyanovna, as a traitor and declared her unworthy to be his aunt. In addition, records confirm that he maintained close ties with ████ over a decade ago. Although ████ personally shot him dead during the rescue operation, we still recommend that a covert investigation be launched into ████...
Through Her Majesty's benevolence and the aid of Grand Duke Pyotr-Lyudvig Pozwisdowitsch of the Strivozha family, the remnants of this rebel organization have now been largely eliminated.

Attachment: Investigation Concerning the Missing Persons
On the day of the incident, witnesses in Snezhnograd reported seeing a human and a fae locked in fierce combat near the Kresnik's Torch. The two were ultimately believed to have leaped into the torch together.
A witness later recovered a longsword nearby. The weapon has since been confirmed as one crafted by President Izmaylov of the Royal Energy Commission, who had previously gifted it to a citizen in a personal capacity. The human and fae spotted by witnesses were in all likelihood the civilian volunteer Anatol and the rebel leader Natalia.
The Transport Bureau has now issued bonuses to the civilian volunteers. A separate, additional compensation payment was also granted to the missing Anatol's sister, Manechka, who also took part in the rescue operation.
It should be noted that the names of Anatol and Manechka appear to have some clout in Snezhnograd's Zolnyy District. Authorities should keep a close watch on activity in the Zolnyy District for the time being, and Manechka should be placed under surveillance as needed.
