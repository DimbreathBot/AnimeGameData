Case File No.: GANO. F. Z13. Op. 3. D. 1104
Subject: Concerning the Discovery of the Body of Theater Actress Karenina Vasilyevna
Case Status: Closed

Report
At five o'clock in the evening on August 14 of this year, a report was received from S.{NON_BREAK_SPACE}A.{NON_BREAK_SPACE}Oblonsky, a citizen employed as a train attendant by the Snezhnaya Royal Railway Transport Bureau, stating that a woman's body had been found on the tracks in Novokitezhgrad. Officers were immediately dispatched to the scene. Upon identification, the deceased was confirmed to be Karenina Vasilyevna, former prima donna of the Novokitezhgrad Theater. This report is hereby submitted for Your Excellency's attention, with a request that a forensic investigator be assigned to the case.

Examination Record
The deceased, clad in a pure white wedding gown, fell from the platform at Novokitezhgrad and was run over by a train pulling into the station, dying instantly.
The investigation has reached the following conclusions:

I.{NON_BREAK_SPACE}The cause of death was impact with and crushing by a train.
II.{NON_BREAK_SPACE}No other injuries indicative of violence inflicted by another person were found.\nOnly traces of sedative medication were detected in the stomach; no hallucinogenic substances were found.
III.{NON_BREAK_SPACE}Only the deceased's footprints were found on the platform. There were no signs of pushing, dragging, or struggling.
IV.{NON_BREAK_SPACE}No Vilas were present at the scene at the time of the incident, thereby ruling out the possibility that the perpetrator had used levitation to commit the crime.
V.{NON_BREAK_SPACE}In the days preceding the incident, the deceased was seen repeatedly wandering the platform in a dazed state and singing to herself.

In light of the findings of the on-site examination, the death shall be ruled a suicide.

Case Closure Report
No criminal offense has been committed. The deceased took her own life while in a state of mental derangement. As no crime was established, the case is closed and filed. The deceased's former husband shall be responsible for her burial.
