Ley Lines themselves possess a powerful ability to either "devour" or "guide" souls. In most cases, once the soul and body of the deceased are separated, the soul is drawn directly into the Ley Lines, leaving no room for lingering existence.
However, evidence shows that the connection between the theater area and the Ley Lines has been obstructed, though only partially, not completely severed. Still, this has granted certain souls unwilling to return to the Ley Lines an opportunity: if their resistance is strong enough, they may even linger within the theater indefinitely.
In theory, however, these souls within the theater are incapable of influencing the physical world. Only under extremely rare circumstances can they produce phenomena observable to the human eye. Overall, they can be considered harmless. This assessment may be conveyed in advance to the theater's management to reassure them.
