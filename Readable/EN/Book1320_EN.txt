A Selection of Excellent Children's Poetry
"Night Journey"
<color=#f39000>A brazier's glow fades into the mountains</color>,
<color=#f39000>a lone lamp sways at the edge of the sky</color>.
<color=#f39000>Worn shoes tread the path before the door</color>,
<color=#f39000>an old tree bends beneath the wind beyond the wall</color>.
<color=#f39000>Crystal shards blanket the land beneath the open sky</color>,
<color=#f39000>guiding travelers along their way till dawn</color>.
