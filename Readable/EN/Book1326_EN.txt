To Your Highness, Grand Duke Alvis:
I must first offer you my congratulations. Were it not for your exceptional foresight, the scoundrels who sought to plunder the Empire's vital research assets might well have succeeded. Now their ringleader, the legendary thief Reed Miller, has at last been brought to justice, and by all accounts the public execution presided over by your own hand became the talk of the capital. Such a remarkable triumph is to be credited largely to your brilliant resolve. For my part, I count myself profoundly fortunate to serve as a mere footnote to your illustrious deeds.
...Reflecting upon the lofty pronouncements you delivered before the Imperial Council that day, the memory remains vivid in my mind. Yet I feel compelled to point out that, however our chosen paths may diverge, both the work at Hvergelmir and my own research on SAMPO are stepping stones laid toward His Majesty's supreme enterprise. His Majesty has already seen fit to entrust all research concerning Hyperborea across the borderlands entirely to me, and I trust you harbor no resentment on that account.
...Of course, my own labors pale into insignificance beside the swift strides Hvergelmir has made in probing the Shadow Realm and the Embryonic-Ichoric-Telluric-Rudiment. Yet should this modest inquiry achieve any small progress under the aegis of His Majesty's glory, I imagine it would prove of no small benefit to that very future you have so long yearned to reach.
...The knell of doom shall soon toll. We shall cross the shattered galleries and return to the deepest star in the night sky, while the fae shall inherit an untainted, boundless earth. As scions of pure light, you and your noble kindred are fully entitled to assert as much. And when that day arrives, I believe it will surely mark the consummate fulfillment of cosmic justice.

Your most faithful friend,
Rotwang
