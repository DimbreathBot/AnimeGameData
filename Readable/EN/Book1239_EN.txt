The snow buries every story. Or perhaps it would be more fitting to say that the snow itself is an endless story.\nSnow falls over Snezhnaya all year round. It buries everything, leaving behind nothing but a blank expanse. Yet beneath that snow lie bones and graves, lives that once burned with untamed vitality. They gave everything in the hope that someone would bear witness to them... Should they not be seen?\nIf I, Vasiliy, succeed, and you, as one of Izmaylov, live to witness this "New Melody" — then it will mean our great undertaking has taken its first step.\nIt will mean that I have secured for all at the "High Table" a place to stand as equals alongside those so-called "great figures."\nI hope that when that day comes, none of you will have to remain hidden in the shadows any longer, living only as the foil to those who bask in the light. You will no longer have to bow and scrape or humble yourselves before others.\nInstead, you will stand tall and proudly call yourselves "Izmaylov."\nI long with all my heart for that future to come. And I, Vasiliy, will lead you there.
